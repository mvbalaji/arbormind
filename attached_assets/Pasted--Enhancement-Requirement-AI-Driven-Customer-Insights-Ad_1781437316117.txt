📌 Enhancement Requirement: AI‑Driven Customer Insights & Advanced Lead Scoring
🎯 Objective
Enhance the Lead Management module so the system can automatically analyse B2B customer information (website, social media, digital footprint) and determine whether the customer is a potential buyer.
Improve the lead scoring mechanism to identify high‑value prospects early and convert them into opportunities faster.

1. AI‑Driven Customer Insight Capture (B2B)
When a Lead is created OR an enquiry is received:

The system must automatically fetch and analyse publicly available customer information from:

Company website

LinkedIn (company + key contacts)

Social media presence (Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube)

Industry directories

Business listings (Google Business, Crunchbase, etc.)

The system should extract insights such as:

Company size (employees, revenue range)

Industry and market segment

Product/service offerings

Recent news, funding, expansions

Hiring trends (indicates growth)

Technology stack (if detectable)

Engagement level on social media

Customer sentiment (positive/neutral/negative)

These insights must be stored in a Customer Insights section within the Lead record.

2. Potential Buyer Identification (AI Classification)
Based on the extracted insights, the system must classify the lead as:

High Potential Buyer

Medium Potential Buyer

Low Potential Buyer

Classification should be based on:

Company growth indicators

Industry relevance

Buying intent signals

Engagement level

Fit with product/service

Historical patterns from similar customers

This classification must be visible on the Lead screen.

3. Enhanced Lead Scoring Mechanism
Upgrade the existing lead scoring model to be more accurate and predictive.

Lead Score Inputs
Customer insights (AI‑generated)

Website behaviour (if available)

Email engagement (open/click rates)

Social media signals

Lead source quality

Completeness of lead information

Historical conversion patterns

Industry conversion probability

Contact seniority (decision maker vs influencer)

Lead Score Output
Score range: 0–100

Score category:

Hot Lead (70–100)

Warm Lead (40–69)

Cold Lead (0–39)

Behaviour
High‑scoring leads should be automatically highlighted.

Optionally auto‑convert Hot Leads into Opportunities (configurable).

4. Early Opportunity Identification
When a lead crosses a configurable score threshold (e.g., 70+):

System should recommend “Convert to Opportunity”

Or auto‑convert (if enabled in configuration)

Notify the Sales Owner

Add the lead to a High‑Potential Pipeline dashboard

5. Business Value
Identifies high‑value customers early

Reduces manual research time

Improves sales conversion rate

Prioritises leads based on real‑world signals

Helps sales teams focus on the right customers

Strengthens CRM intelligence and automation
